User Guide

PRODUCT AnuRupa Matrimony
VERSION 2.0
DATE 5 October 2026

AnuRupa Matrimony is a private matchmaking platform for Anupama Reddy. It holds your profile book, lets you search it for a named client, and share a shortlist with that client over WhatsApp — then tells you what they opened and which profiles they liked.

The admin side is yours and is password protected. The client side is what families see: a share link you send them, or the public browse page. Clients never see your internal notes, finances or tags.

1How it works

A typical day with the platform is five steps:

  1. Add profiles as families come to you, using the intake form.
  2. Search the book on behalf of a client, filtering by caste, job, age, height and finances.
  3. Tick the matches and choose how much to reveal — photos only, basic or all details.
  4. Send one link on WhatsApp. The client opens it on their phone, no login needed.
  5. Watch what comes back — which links were opened and which profiles were hearted.

Three kinds of people use it, and each sees a different slice:

WhoWhat they doWhat they see
You (admin)Sign in, add and edit profiles, search, share, follow up.Everything.
A family with a linkOpen the WhatsApp link, browse the shortlist, heart the ones they like.Only the profiles you chose, at the level you chose.
A walk-inOpen the public browse page, give name and mobile, filter and heart.Public profiles, basic details only.
Everyone becomes one client record, matched by phone number. A family that hearts a profile is asked for a name and mobile once; a walk-in gives it before anything is shown. Someone who browses today and gets a WhatsApp link next week is one client with one history.

2Signing in & the home screen

Go to /admin and enter the admin password. The session lasts 12 hours. You land on six sections — New Profile, Search, All Profiles, Dashboard, Follow-up and Journey — with Clients, Share Links, Settings and Deleted underneath. Everything starts from here.

Sign in with the admin password.
The home screen.

3Adding a profile

The form follows your paper intake sheet, tab by tab. Only Name is mandatory — create a profile from a phone call and fill in the rest when the family sends details.

Personal is laid out like the sheet: the person down the left (name, date and time of birth, place of birth, height in feet and inches, native place) and their lineage down the right (surname, caste, sub-caste, gotram, nakshatram, rasi, religion, mother tongue). Current location is a country list — India, Europe, Africa, USA, Australia — and picking India or the USA turns the state field into the proper list of states.

Personal — the person on the left, lineage on the right.

Professional starts with a job category — Doctor, Engineer, Software Engineer, Business or Others. That is what the job filter searches on; the free-text job field stays for the detail, so you can record "Civil Engineer at L&T" and still find them under Engineer.

Job category drives the search filter.

Family puts the father on the left and the mother on the right, then gives each of two siblings a name, a marital status and a Potential client box. Tick that box for an unmarried sibling you might end up matchmaking for next — those profiles can then be pulled up as a list of their own.

Two siblings, each with a status and a potential-client box.

Contact keeps the family and the middleman apart: primary contact name, their relation to the person, and their number on one side; the middleman's name and number on the other. Everything about the middleman is admin-only and is never shared, at any level.

The family's contact and the middleman's, kept separate.

Requirements holds what the family is looking for, plus an Urgent flag for profiles that need pushing. Internal holds the Anurupa tag — tick any of AM, AMP and AMO, not just one — an Anurupa Aura flag, net worth and your private notes. None of it ever reaches a client.

Internal — tags, Aura, and notes only you see.

Photos: click Choose photos, drag images onto the dotted box, or copy an image and press ⌘V. The first photo is the cover; after saving you can reorder, change the cover or delete.

Editing later: open All Profiles to see everything on the books, newest first. Click a card to edit any field or photo. Urgent and Aura profiles are badged on the card.

Click a card to open and edit it.

4Searching for a client

Search always runs on behalf of someone — that is what makes the tracking work. Pick an existing client or add a new one with a name and phone number.

Choose the client first.

Every filter is on screen at once — nothing is hidden behind another choice. Boy or girl, year of birth, caste, job, the Anurupa tags, more-than / less-than ranges for age, height and finances, and tick-boxes for Urgent only, Potential clients and Anurupa Aura.

All Profiles — every filter visible together.

Results show just what you need to judge a match at a glance — name, date of birth and age, height, caste, requirements — with View / edit all details for the full record.

Already-shared profiles go grey and are badged Already shared, so you never send the same profile to a client twice. Profiles they hearted are badged Liked.
Search results for a client. Grey cards were already sent to them.

5Sharing a shortlist

Tick the profiles you want (or Select all) and press Share. One link covers everything selected, so the family gets a single WhatsApp message. Choose how much they see:

LevelWhat the client sees
Photos onlyName and the photo gallery. Nothing else.
Basic detailsName (no surname), birth details, height, job, business, native place, photos.
All detailsEverything above plus surname, caste, education, family, siblings, requirements and the family's contact.

Set how many days the link stays alive, add an optional label, and generate. You get a Copy link button and a Share on WhatsApp button that opens WhatsApp with the message already written and addressed to the client by name.

Finances, the tags, the Aura and Urgent flags and the middleman's details are never shared — not even on "All details". They stay on your side only.

6Slideshow

After any search, press Slideshow to run through the results full screen, in the order they are on the page. It is on All Profiles, on Search, and on the public browse page.

Each slide is the photo, the name, and a heart. It starts playing on its own and moves on every few seconds; the arrows, the dots along the bottom or the left and right arrow keys move it by hand, which pauses it so it stops moving under you. Escape or Close ends it.

One profile at a time — photo, name, heart.
The heart is the same shortlist as the one on the cards. On Search it records against the client you are searching for; on browse it records against the family looking. All Profiles has no heart, because it is not scoped to a client, so there would be nobody to record it against.

7What the client sees

From a WhatsApp link: the profiles you chose, at the level you chose, with a heart on each. No login, no app. If you named the client when creating the link, their hearts are recorded straight away. If you did not, the first heart asks for a name and mobile — links get forwarded within families, so this records who actually liked it.

A shared link on the client's side.

From the public browse page: they must give a name and phone number before anything is shown, and they are asked every time they start browsing from the home page — so a shared phone or a family laptop never records one person's likes against another's. Then they can filter, heart and run the slideshow, and every search is recorded against them.

Nothing is visible until they identify themselves.
Then they browse and heart.

8Keeping track

Clients lists everyone you have shared with or who has browsed. Open one to see what they were sent, what they shortlisted, what they searched for, and whether each link was opened. You can log a follow-up against them, remove a shortlist, or delete a link from here.

One client — sent, liked, searched, called.

Dashboard answers four questions: who you contacted in the last 7 days, who has been silent for 30+ days (worth a call), who never opened a link, and who was never contacted at all — people whose details you took and then never sent anything to, oldest first. Each of those rows has a delete control, so the dashboard doubles as the place to tidy up. "Responded" means they opened a link or hearted a profile; your own calls never count as their response.

The dashboard. Each name links to that client.

Share Links lists every link ever created, who it went to, how often it was opened and when it expires. You can copy it, re-send on WhatsApp, add +3 days, revoke it immediately, or delete it outright. Revoking keeps the row so you can still see it was sent; deleting is for links sent by mistake.

Extend, re-share, revoke or delete a link.

9Follow-up notes

Follow-up gives Anupama, Shaurya and Shreya a running list each. Type a bullet, press Enter for the next one, and press Save. The card tells you whether it is up to date or has unsaved changes, so nothing is lost by accident.

These are your own working notes, kept apart from the per-client follow-ups on a client's page — use those to record what was said on a specific call, and these for everything else.

A running list for each of you.

10Journey

Journey holds photos and videos from the matches you have made. Choose files or drag them onto the box, add an optional caption, and upload. Each item can be downloaded again or deleted.

Like profile photos, these are held in private storage and served through links that expire after an hour — nothing is publicly reachable.

Photos and videos from the matches made.

11Deleting & restoring

Deleting a profile or a client asks you how:

ChoiceWhat happens
Move to DeletedHidden from every screen, including live share links, but nothing is destroyed. Restore it at any time and it comes back exactly as it was.
Delete permanentlyGone for good, along with its photos, shortlists and links. This cannot be undone, and the button tells you what it will take with it.

The Deleted page lists everything you have moved there, with Restore and Delete permanently beside each. The Dashboard shows how many are waiting.

Deleted — restore, or remove for good.
A soft-deleted profile disappears from links you have already sent, straight away. If a family asks you to pull a profile back, move it to Deleted and the links they are holding stop showing it.

Smaller things — a follow-up note, a shortlist entry, a share link, a Journey file — are deleted in one step with a confirm, since there is nothing meaningful to restore for a one-line note.

12Changing the colours

Settings holds the six colours the whole platform is built from. Change one and everything follows — the admin pages, the browse page and every link you have already sent — with no redeploy and nothing to reinstall.

Backgrounds and hover shades are worked out from the primary colour, and the badge text shade is darkened from the gold until it can be read, so a palette can never end up half-applied. A preview beside the fields shows the effect before you save, and Reset to defaults puts the original palette back.

Six colours; everything else is worked out from them.
