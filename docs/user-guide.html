User Guide

PRODUCT AnuRupa Matrimony
VERSION 1.1
DATE 2 October 2026

AnuRupa Matrimony is a private matchmaking platform for Anupama Reddy. It holds your profile book, lets you search it for a named client, and share a shortlist with that client over WhatsApp — then tells you what they opened and which profiles they liked.

The admin side is yours and is password protected. The client side is what families see: a share link you send them, or the public browse page. Clients never see your internal notes, finances or tags.

1How it works

A typical day with the platform is five steps:

  1. Add profiles as families come to you, using the intake form.
  2. Search the book on behalf of a client, filtering by caste, age, height and finances.
  3. Tick the matches and choose how much to reveal — photos only, basic or all details.
  4. Send one link on WhatsApp. The client opens it on their phone, no login needed.
  5. Watch what comes back — which links were opened and which profiles were hearted.

Three kinds of people use it, and each sees a different slice:

WhoWhat they doWhat they see
You (admin)Sign in, add and edit profiles, search, share, follow up.Everything.
A family with a linkOpen the WhatsApp link, browse the shortlist, heart the ones they like.Only the profiles you chose, at the level you chose.

Everyone becomes one client record, matched by phone number. A family that hearts a profile is asked for a name and mobile once; a walk-in gives it before anything is shown. Someone who browses today and gets a WhatsApp link next week is one client with one history.

2Signing in & the home screen

Go to /admin and enter the admin password. The session lasts 12 hours. You land on four sections — New Profile, Search, All Profiles and Dashboard — with Clients and Share Links underneath. Everything starts from here.

Sign in with the admin password.

3Adding a profile

The form follows your paper intake sheet, tab by tab: Personal (name, birth details, rasi, nakshatram, gotram, caste, height, city), Education, Professional (job, company, salary, finances), Family, Contact, Requirements and Internal (tag, net worth, private notes). Only Name is mandatory — create a profile from a phone call and fill in the rest when the family sends details.

The intake form, in the same sections as your sheet.

Photos: click Choose photos, drag images onto the dotted box, or copy an image and press ⌘V. The first photo is the cover; after saving you can reorder, change the cover or delete.

Editing later: open All Profiles to see everything on the books, newest first. Click a card to edit any field or photo.

All Profiles — click a card to open and edit it.

4Searching for a client

Search always runs on behalf of someone — that is what makes the tracking work. Pick an existing client or add a new one with a name and phone number.

Choose the client first.

Then filter: name, looking for, caste, tag, and more-than / less-than ranges for age, height and finances. Results show just what you need to judge a match at a glance — name, date of birth and age, height, caste, requirements — with View / edit all details for the full record.

Already-shared profiles go grey and are badged Already shared, so you never send the same profile to a client twice. Profiles they hearted are badged Liked.
Search results for a client. Grey cards were already sent to them.

5Sharing a shortlist

Tick the profiles you want (or Select all) and press Share. One link covers everything selected, so the family gets a single WhatsApp message. Choose how much they see:

LevelWhat the client sees
Photos onlyName and the photo gallery. Nothing else.
Basic detailsName (no surname), birth details, height, job, business, native place, photos.
All detailsEverything above plus surname, caste, education, family, horoscope, requirements and contact.

Set how many days the link stays alive, add an optional label, and generate. You get a Copy link button and a Share on WhatsApp button that opens WhatsApp with the message already written and addressed to the client by name.

Finances, marital status and the tag are never shared — not even on "All details". They stay on your side only.

6What the client sees

From a WhatsApp link: the profiles you chose, at the level you chose, with a heart on each. No login, no app. The first heart asks for their name and mobile — links get forwarded within families, so this records who actually liked it. After that, hearts are one tap.

A shared link on the client's side.

From the public browse page: they must give a name and phone number before anything is shown. Then they can filter and heart profiles just like a shared link, and every search is recorded against them.

Nothing is visible until they identify themselves.
Then they browse and heart.

7Keeping track

Clients lists everyone you have shared with or who has browsed. Open one to see what they were sent, what they shortlisted, what they searched for, and whether each link was opened.

One client — sent, liked, searched.

Dashboard answers three questions: who you contacted in the last 7 days, who has been silent for 30+ days (worth a call), and who never opened a link. "Responded" means they opened a link or hearted a profile.

The dashboard. Each name links to that client.

Share Links lists every link ever created, who it went to, how often it was opened and when it expires. You can copy it, re-send on WhatsApp, add +3 days, or revoke it immediately.

Extend, re-share or revoke a link.
